Product Story

A masterpiece of regal elegance, the Grand Lapis Lazuli & Gold Candelabra Lamp represents the height of luxury décor and old-world craftsmanship. Inspired by European palace lighting traditions, this statement piece blends the richness of natural Lapis Lazuli with ornate gold detailing to create a sculptural lighting accessory worthy of royal interiors.

Its opulent eight-arm structure, crowned with refined fabric lampshades, evokes the grandeur of classical chandeliers while standing as a collectable object d'art. Designed for connoisseurs of fine décor, this lamp transforms any room into a setting of prestige, sophistication, and timeless beauty.

Whether placed in a grand foyer, formal living room, luxury suite, or private office, this extraordinary lamp elevates the space with its commanding presence and jewel-like finish.

Product Description

This exquisite lamp features a handcrafted Lapis Lazuli vase base, meticulously shaped and polished to showcase the gemstone's signature deep-green mosaic patterning. Each stone fragment is inlaid by master artisans to achieve a flawless finish.

The centerpiece rises into a beautifully sculpted, solid brass candelabra, finished in radiant gold plating. Its eight gracefully curved arms are adorned with detailed Baroque-style engravings and topped with premium pleated white lampshades, adding a soft, diffused glow that enhances the warmth of the gold.

Every element — from the Lapis Lazuli vase to the intricate brass motifs — showcases artistry at its finest, making this lamp not just a lighting object, but a significant collector's décor piece.
